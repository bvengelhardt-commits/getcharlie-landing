Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Overview

Charlie's Recos is an app that allows users to save, share, and discover recommendations for places like restaurants, hotels, and attractions.

2. Data We Collect

  • Account data: Email address and authentication information
  • User content: Recommendations, comments, ratings, and photos you upload
  • Location data: Only when using location features
  • Contacts (optional): Only with explicit consent for friend search
  • Usage data: Basic analytics to improve features

3. How We Use Data

  • Providing and operating the app
  • Enabling social features like sharing and recommendations
  • Storing and displaying uploaded photos
  • Improving app quality and performance

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Information is only shared with other users according to your visibility settings.

5. Data Storage

Data is stored securely via trusted third-party infrastructure (e.g. Firebase / Google Cloud).

6. Account Deletion

You can request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time:

getcharlie.app/delete-account

All personal data will be permanently deleted within 30 days of the request.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to request access, correction, or deletion of your personal data at any time.

8. Travelpayouts Drive (Affiliate Links)

This website uses "Travelpayouts Drive", a script from the travel affiliate network Travelpayouts. It automatically detects links to certain travel providers (e.g. hotels, activities) and replaces them with partner links, through which we earn a commission on successful bookings — this doesn't change the price for you.

Purpose: funding the free service through commissions.

Legal basis: legitimate interest in funding the service (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Recipients: Travelpayouts and the respective booking provider once a partner link is clicked. This may involve a data transfer outside the EU/EEA (including the US).

Retention: cookies used to attribute a booking to a click are set for a period defined by Travelpayouts (typically a few days to a few weeks).

You can object to this processing by blocking third-party scripts/cookies in your browser — the website continues to work fully without it.
