Frequently Asked Questions

  • How do I add a place? Tap the + button on the map, search for a location, and save it with a note and rating.
  • Who can see my recommendations? You control visibility for each place — just you, friends only, or everyone.
  • How do I find friends? Go to the Friends tab and search by name or email address.
  • How do I import from Google Maps? Go to Profile → Import from Google Maps, and upload your Google Takeout ZIP file.
  • How do I delete my account? See our account deletion page or contact us at hello@charliesrecos.com.

Privacy

Charlie does not sell your data. Contacts are only read locally on your device to find friends — no contact data is uploaded to any server. See our full Privacy Policy.
